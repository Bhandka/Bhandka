## 👋 About

Materials engineering graduate who runs production equipment, characterizes the results, and builds the Python tools that close the loop between the two.

## 🎯 Focus areas

### 🏭 Process & equipment engineering
- Hands-on operation and lead responsibility for thin-film and nanomaterial production
- Yield and defect excursion management, fault detection and classification (FDC), and equipment qualification
- Troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and documentation
- Lab engineering in a large-scale technology environment

### 📊 Statistical process control & quality
- Control charts (X-bar/R, I-MR), process capability (Cp/Cpk), and trend and drift detection
- Measurement system analysis: Gauge R&R, bias and repeatability studies
- Equipment comparison and tool matching with Bland-Altman analysis and correlation/regression
- Design of Experiments (DOE): factorial designs, ANOVA, and response surface optimization
- Hypothesis testing and process improvement

### 🐍 Data analytics & automation
- Python pipelines for process, lab, and inspection data: cleaning, analysis, reporting
- Statistical comparison of pre- and post-process results
- Dashboards (Streamlit) for operational data

### 🔬 Characterization & simulation
- Microscopy and TEM image analysis (ImageJ/Fiji, Python)
- Finite element modeling with COMSOL
